# LumaSync 0.2.0

## What's new

- Enter custom colors as six-digit hex values in the color picker.
- Preview calibration colors on the LEDs without saving them as the static color.
- Adjust up to three hue ranges independently, each with separate red, green, and blue gains.
- Hue correction defaults to neutral 100% gains. Use the controls to compensate for colors your LED strip or controller reproduces unevenly; **Neutral RGB** restores all gains to 100% without changing hue ranges.
- The built-in palette now includes red, green, blue, cyan, magenta, yellow, violet, and pink calibration colors.
- Settings migrate to schema 9 while retaining saved settings.
